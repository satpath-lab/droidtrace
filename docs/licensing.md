# Licensing notes
The project is Apache-2.0. The QEMU plugin is compiled against `qemu-plugin.h` and loaded into a
GPL-2.0-or-later QEMU process. The files in `qemu-patch/` are compiled into QEMU and are GPL-2.0-or-later; a QEMU binary
built with them must be distributed under the GPL. Have your legal team confirm the distribution model for binary
plugin builds within your organisation.
